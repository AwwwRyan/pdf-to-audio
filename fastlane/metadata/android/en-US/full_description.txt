Accha Sunno turns text-based PDFs into audio you can listen to anywhere, with the screen off. Notes, study guides, reports and books all work. Everything happens on your phone, using Android's built-in text-to-speech engine. There are no accounts, no cloud services and no network calls.

<b>Made for listening</b>
• Running headers, footers and page numbers are removed, so they aren't read aloud on every page
• Headings get their own pause, and paragraphs keep their natural breaks
• Code blocks are replaced with "Code example skipped." You can turn this off in Settings
• Symbols like → and abbreviations like "e.g." are read naturally

<b>Player</b>
• Follow-along text shows the passage being spoken. Tap any passage to jump to it
• Skip back 30, 10 or 5 seconds, or forward 5, 10 or 30 seconds
• Playback speed from 0.75× to 2×
• Keeps playing with the screen locked, with notification, lock-screen and headphone controls
• Remembers where you stopped in every document

<b>Your library</b>
• Convert a whole PDF or just a page range
• Conversion can resume if it's interrupted
• See how much storage each document uses, and delete the audio while keeping the document

<b>Voices</b>
Pick any voice installed on your phone, and set the speech rate and pitch. For the best results, install offline voice data from Android's text-to-speech settings. The app links straight there.

Scanned PDFs (images of pages) aren't supported, because they contain no text to read.
